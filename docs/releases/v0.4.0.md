# Invoice Maker v0.4.0

Car mileage reports, Vehicle and General/Admin sections on expense reports, and safer autosave when you move between pages.

## New

### Car mileage reports

A new **Car Mileage** area in the sidebar keeps a standalone, separately numbered mileage report.

- Enter the vehicle, then one row per trip: **date**, **from**, **to**, **purpose**, and the **start and end odometer** in whole kilometres. Each trip's distance and the report total are calculated for you.
- Drafts autosave and reopen where you left off. A blank odometer reading is allowed while you work, and is not treated as zero.
- **Preview** shows the report as it will print. **Lock & Save** numbers it, keeps a permanent copy that always reprints exactly as locked, and saves a PDF in that year's `YYYY-mileage` folder.
- **Car Mileage → History** lets you view a locked report, save its PDF again, cancel it, or restore a cancelled report.
- Reports record distances only. They do not calculate reimbursement, per-kilometre, tax, or legal rates.

### Vehicle and General/Admin expense sections

- Expense reports now have two sections, **Vehicle expenses** and **General/Admin expenses**. Each shows its own subtotal, and the report total adds the two together.
- Put a row in the wrong section? Its arrow button moves it to the other section with everything you typed.
- **Sort rows by date, then description** and **Lock & Save** order each section by date, with same-day rows ordered by description.

## Improvements and fixes

- **Leaving a page never drops unsaved changes.** If a save fails, the editor stays on screen with your changes, keeps retrying in the background, and explains why it didn't switch pages. Try again once it says **Saved**. Once a page change is allowed, the editor locks while the next page loads, so nothing typed in that moment can be lost.
- **Switching or discarding saved invoice drafts** first saves the invoice you have open. If that save fails, nothing is switched or discarded.
- **Keyboard focus** in the expense and mileage editors now follows your work. Adding a row moves you to its date. Removing a row moves you to **Undo**, and undoing returns you to the restored row.
- **Preview** in the expense and mileage editors works as a proper dialog. Focus starts on **Close preview**, Tab stays inside the preview, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the **Preview** button.
- **Backups and restore** include mileage reports. The restore summary shows how many locked mileage reports a backup contains.

## Your existing records

- Invoices, expense reports, and their PDFs that you already locked reprint exactly as before. Expense reports locked before sections existed keep their original single list of rows.
- Rows in an expense report that was still unfinished start in **General/Admin expenses**. Move any vehicle costs with the arrow button before you lock it.
- **Automatic upgrade, no setup needed.** The first time v0.4.0 opens, it upgrades its local database before you start. It adds the expense section setting, with existing rows set to General/Admin, and the mileage report storage. All existing data is kept. Each upgrade step either completes fully or leaves your data as it was. A backup from an earlier version is upgraded the same way when you restore it.
- Earlier versions of the app won't restore a backup made by v0.4.0. If you want a copy from before the upgrade, use **Settings → Backups → Back up now** before installing.

## Install or update

**Already installed?** The app offers the update when it starts, or use **Settings → About & updates → Check for updates**.

**Windows:** download the `.exe` or `.msi` installer below and run it. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

**macOS (Apple silicon and Intel):** download the file ending in `_universal.dmg`. Open the DMG and drag **Invoice Maker** to **Applications**, then open it from Applications. The app is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized. On first launch macOS may block it. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway** for Invoice Maker, and confirm **Open**. This is a one-time approval for this installation. See the [Mac installation guide](https://github.com/jordanvanallen/invoice-app/blob/main/USER_GUIDE.md#macos) for details.
